# Prompt for AI UI Feedback - Mattress Competitor Comparator

## Copy and paste this to your AI:

---

**CONTEXT:**

I'm a mattress salesperson at Mattress Firm. I built this web app to help me close more sales by comparing our products against competitors during customer presentations.

**App Purpose:**
- Show Mattress Firm products and highlight where we beat competitors
- Build trust and justify why customers should buy from us
- Use during the sales pitch to overcome "I can find it cheaper elsewhere" objections

**Features:**
- Select a Mattress Firm product from a list
- Compare against 5 competitors (Carol House Furniture, Mattress Warehouse, Sleep Number, Wayfair, Amazon)
- Compare on 5 metrics: Price, Financing APR, Financing Term, Warranty, Delivery time
- Show "WE BEAT THEM" badges highlighting our advantages
- Green highlighting for wins

**Target Users:**
- Mattress Firm salespeople using on tablets/laptops during in-store appointments
- Customers watching on screen during pitch

**Design Constraints:**
- Must work on tablet (primary use case)
- Must be readable from a few feet away (customer viewing)
- Simple, bold, high-contrast for quick scanning
- Navy/dark background with bright accent colors

---

**TASK:**

Review the screenshots I'm about to share and provide feedback on:
1. What's wrong with the current UI/UX?
2. What improvements would make it better for closing sales?
3. Any usability issues for the salesperson or customer?
4. Suggestions for making the "WE BEAT THEM" message more impactful?

Be specific and actionable. This is for a real sales environment.
