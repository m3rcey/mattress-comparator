# Fiverr Job Post - Mattress Price Research

---

## Title:
Mattress Price Research - Create Spreadsheet of Competitor Prices

---

## Description:
I need someone to research and compile mattress prices from major retailers into an organized spreadsheet.

---

## What I'll Provide:
- List of mattress brands and models to research
- List of competitor websites to check

---

## What You Need to Do:
1. Visit each competitor website
2. Search for each mattress model in **ALL available sizes**
3. Record the current price for each size
4. Note if a competitor doesn't carry that brand/model
5. Put all results in a clean Excel/Google Sheets spreadsheet
